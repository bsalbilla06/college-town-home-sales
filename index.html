How these numbers were made

  • Implausible prices are dropped. We keep sales from … to …. That removes … sales under the floor (including one at $0) and … above the ceiling (up to $81.5 million), for … of … rows.
  • Price per square foot uses only homes with a living area above zero; 127 rows report zero square feet and cannot be divided.
  • Days on market is the gap from listing to accepted offer, not to closing. Closing adds roughly a month. We do not treat a missing date as zero days.
  • No de-duplication by address. 66 rows share a street address, mostly condo units in one State College building. Each is a separate sale with its own listing id.
  • Home type is ignored. The source labels nearly every row "single family", including condo units, so we do not filter or group by it.
  • Medians, not means. A handful of very large or unusual properties would pull averages upward, so every headline figure here is a median.

Figures are recomputed from the raw CSV by scripts/build_site_data.py, which writes data/derived/site_data.json. The five original dataset files in data/ are unchanged.
