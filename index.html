Data and methods

Rules applied here

Price scope
Questions 1 and 4 to 8 and 10 use sales with a sale_price from $10,000 to $5,000,000 inclusive. That is 35,371 of 35,442 rows. It removes 40 sales under $10,000, including one at $0, and 31 above $5,000,000, up to $81,510,000. Questions 2, 3 and 9 also use all rows, as the assignment asks.
Campus area and city
A campus area is the dataset's university column, 11 values. A city is city and state together, 18 values, such as Champaign, IL.
Days on market
contract_date minus list_date, in days, the listing-to-accepted-offer measure real estate sites use. Only sales with both dates count; a blank date is never read as zero. That leaves 79.2% of sales.
Missing values
A missing value is left out of the statistic, never filled with zero. Filters that ask about garage, basement or fireplace offer Unknown as its own choice, and the range filters exclude sales with no recorded value.
Medians, not means
Every question uses the median unless it asks for the mean, so a handful of unusual properties cannot move the answer.
No de-duplication by address
66 rows share a street address, mostly condo units in one State College building. Each is a separate sale with its own listing_id, and all are kept.
Chart types
This page uses horizontal bar (Q1, Q4, Q6, Q7, Q8), diverging bar (Q2), slope (Q3), dot plot with quartile ranges (Q5), monthly column (Q9) and histogram (Q10), plus a grouped bar in the insights.

Where the numbers come from

The site ships one row-level extract, data/derived/site_data.json, made from the dataset by scripts/build_site_data.py. Every figure on this page is computed in your browser from those rows, so a setting you choose is answered from the full data rather than from a precomputed number. A separate script, scripts/answer_key.py, recomputes the graded answers with pandas so they can be checked against the site.

Values are as reported by the collection source and were not verified against county records. Treat them as a copy of what one source said in March 2026.

Citation. College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
